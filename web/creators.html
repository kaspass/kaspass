How setup works

For now, we set up each page with you. It takes one short conversation and a few approvals in your wallet.

  1. Tell us about your plans

    What you'd like to sell, your prices, what each plan includes, and your website, so your page can match it.

  2. Approve the setup in Kaspire

    Your wallet creates your vault and your plans. You approve each step, so everything belongs to your wallet from the start.

  3. Share your link

    Put it on your website, in your app or in your posts. Members who already have a Kaspa wallet join in about a minute.

What you'll need

  • The free Kaspire wallet, on a computer with Chrome or on an Android phone. You sign in and get paid with it.
  • A second wallet as a backup, used only if you ever lose the first. We'll show you how to make one.
  • A few KAS for setup: small deposits that stay yours, tiny network fees, and some for your fees wallet, which pays the network fee when a membership renews itself.
  • Your name, logo and colors, or just your website's address: we can copy the look from it.
  • Your plans: what each is called, what it includes, and what it costs a month or a year.

It sounds like a lot. We do it with you, step by step.
