For creators

Get your own membership page.

Sell memberships for your app, community or content, paid in KAS straight to your wallet. Send us a short note below: we set your page up with you, in your own look, and usually reply within a couple of days.

KasPass takes 2% of each payment (nothing on payments under 25 KAS). No monthly fee. Setup costs a few KAS in deposits. You'll need the free Kaspire wallet first.

What you get

A page that looks like yours

Your colors, fonts, logo, photo and banner, with day and night modes. A link back to your site.

Your own vault

Payments go to a vault on Kaspa that only your wallet can empty. Nobody else holds your money.

Levels and plans

Several levels, each with monthly, commitment and lifetime prices, free trials, founding prices and sales.

Prices that hold steady

Set prices in Canadian or US dollars, euros or KAS. Members pay the KAS amount for that price today.

A check for your site

One call tells your app whether a wallet is a member, and at which level. Members return to your site after paying.

A dashboard

Members, earnings, renewals, prices and your look, in one place, signed in with your wallet.

How setup works

For now, each page is set up by hand, with you.

1

Tell us about your plans

Your levels, prices, what each includes, and your website for the look.

2

Your wallet creates your vault and your plans. You approve each step, so everything belongs to your wallet from the start.

3

Share your link

Put it on your site, in your app or in your posts. Members join in about a minute.
