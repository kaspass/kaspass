Give a membership

For giveaways, staff, or someone who paid you another way. A free trial costs you only its deposit. A full month is paid from your wallet and comes straight back to your vault, less the KasPass fee, so you need the full price in your wallet to start.

Price and sales are set for each plan: pick the plan at the top.

Price

Buyers pay this in KAS at today's rate. A new price applies to new members straight away, and current members pay it at their next renewal.

Advanced: the most KAS a member can be charged

Members never pay more KAS than this ceiling, even if KAS falls a lot. It starts at 3 times the launch day's price. If KAS falls far, raise it; if KAS rises far, you can lower it. Each member keeps the ceiling they joined with. Changing it takes one approval in Kaspire.

Technical details

Launch a new membership

Creating a plan costs 1 KAS, which stays locked in it, plus a tiny network fee. Payments go to your payout vault. Settings marked Permanent can never be changed, so check them before you create it.

KasPass takes 2% of each payment, from your share (none on payments under 25 KAS).

Turn on payouts first. New plans pay into your payout vault, so that comes first.

Fills in everything from that plan: name, what it includes, price and settings. Change what you need, like the name or the free trial, then create it.

About it (optional)
More ways to pay (optional): lifetime, commitment, founding price

These can all be changed later in Prices & sales. A founding price is a lower first payment for people who join before a date.

Advanced settings (most creators leave these as they are)

Members pay your price in KAS at today's rate. The ceiling is the most KAS a payment can ever be: if KAS falls a lot, members pay the ceiling instead. It starts at 3 times today's amount, and you can change it later in Prices & sales.
