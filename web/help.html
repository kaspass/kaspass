Getting started

On a phone

  1. Get the free Kaspire app from kaspire.kaslab.space. It's on Android for now; on an iPhone, use a computer instead (below) until the app arrives.
  2. Create a wallet. Write down the recovery words and keep them somewhere safe and offline. They're the only way back into your wallet, and your membership lives there.
  3. Put some KAS in it (how).
  4. On the membership page, tap Sign in with the Kaspire app. Approve in the app, then come back to the page.

On a computer

  1. Add the free Kaspire wallet to Chrome (or another browser that runs Chrome extensions) from kaspire.kaslab.space.
  2. Create a wallet and write down the recovery words. Keep them safe and offline.
  3. Put some KAS in it (how).
  4. On the membership page, click Sign in with Kaspire. Already have the Kaspire app on your phone? Click Use the Kaspire app on my phone instead and scan the code with the app.

Signing in is free: Kaspire asks you to approve a short message. Nothing is paid.

Joining a plan

  1. Pick a plan. Its price is shown in dollars or euros, with what that is in KAS today.
  2. The box that opens shows what you get, the price, a small refundable deposit and the total. Tick "Prepay future months (auto-renew)" there if you'd like it to renew itself.
  3. Press the pay button. Kaspire shows the same amounts, numbered. Check that they match, then approve.

Your membership appears within a few seconds. The KAS price follows the exchange rate, and a price you're shown is held for 10 minutes.

What is KAS, and where do I get it?

KAS is the coin of the Kaspa network. Plans are priced in dollars or euros, and you pay the same value in KAS.

  1. Buy KAS on a crypto exchange. Most large exchanges sell it.
  2. In Kaspire, open Receive and copy your address.
  3. On the exchange, withdraw (send) the KAS to that address.

Send a little more than the price: each payment has a tiny network fee, and a small deposit (usually 1 KAS) is held in your membership. You get the deposit back when your membership ends.

Free trials

Some plans let you try them free. To start, you set aside your first month inside your own membership (plus 10% in case the KAS price moves) and a small deposit. Nobody else can touch it.

  • Changed your mind? Press Cancel my trial before the trial ends. Everything you set aside comes straight back to your wallet, and you keep access until the trial ends.
  • Staying? Do nothing. At the end of the trial your first month is taken from what you set aside, and you stay a member. Anything left over stays in your membership, and you can take it back any time.

One free trial per person.

Join the members' groups

Once you've joined, the page shows Join the Telegram group and Connect Discord (if the creator has them).

  • Telegram: it opens a chat with the site's bot. Press Start and the bot sends you a one-time invite.
  • Discord: sign in with Discord and you get the members' role.

If your membership ends, you're removed automatically. Renew, then send /status to the Telegram bot to come back (on Discord your role comes back by itself).

Renewing

No subscription. Nothing is taken automatically unless you choose to prepay. When your membership ends, it simply stops.

  • Renew by hand: near the end date a Renew button appears on your plan. If your membership has already ended, renewing brings it back.
  • Prepay future months (auto-renew): set some KAS aside in your own membership. It's held in your membership's own contract on the Kaspa blockchain, not by KasPass or the creator: nobody else can take it out, it can only pay your renewals one at a time, and you can take the rest back any time. You can add a 10% or 25% cushion in case KAS drops. Only keep enough there for your renewals: it isn't a bank account. Shortly before each end date, one month (or year) comes out of it, at that day's KAS price, so it may cover more or fewer months than you planned. If KAS falls and what's left doesn't cover the next one, nothing is taken: you get a message (if you linked Telegram or Discord) and can top up, or it simply ends and the KAS stays yours. Turn it on with Manage your membership, then Prepay future months… (or tick it when you join).
  • Moving to a higher plan: buy the higher plan. The unused part of your current one counts toward it. Afterwards the page offers to end the lower one and give you its deposit back.

Cancel or end a membership

You don't need to do anything to cancel. A membership ends on its end date unless you renew.

  • Stop prepaying (auto-renew): Manage your membership, then Cancel prepaid months. All your unused prepaid KAS comes back to your wallet, and your access stays until the end date. A month already renewed isn't refunded.
  • End it early: Manage your membership, then End membership now. You lose access straight away, and the time you paid for is not refunded. Only your deposit (and any prepaid KAS) comes back. You press twice, so it can't happen by accident.
  • After it has ended: the same place offers Get my deposit back.

Can I get my money back?

Payments go straight to the creator, so KasPass can't refund them. If something went wrong, contact the creator. These always come back to you:

  • your deposit, when you end your membership;
  • any prepaid KAS (auto-renew) you haven't used;
  • a free trial's money, if you cancel before the trial ends.

Give your membership to someone else

If the plan allows it: Manage your membership, then Give it to someone else, and paste their Kaspa address. They get the rest of your time and you lose access. This can't be undone. Some creators receive a small fee when a membership changes hands; the page shows it first.

Pay from another wallet (QR code)

If your KAS is in another wallet or on an exchange, sign in with Kaspire, pick a plan, and choose Pay from another wallet (QR code). Scan the code with that wallet (or copy the address and amount) and send the exact amount. The page finishes by itself.

The membership goes to the wallet you're signed in with. Your payment is held safely until it becomes your membership. If it can't (too little, or too late), it comes back to your signed-in wallet automatically within the hour.

Where your KAS goes

Payments go straight to the creator. KasPass never holds your KAS. Your deposit and any prepaid KAS stay inside your own membership, and only you can take them back.

Trouble?

  • "We couldn't find the Kaspire wallet in this browser": check the extension is installed and turned on, then reload the page. On a phone, use the Kaspire app instead.
  • "You need about … KAS and this wallet has … KAS": add KAS to your wallet (how), then try again.
  • "That price was held for 10 minutes and has run out": press the button again for today's price.
  • "Price unavailable right now": exchange rates couldn't be fetched just now. Try again in a minute.
  • "We're still loading the latest payments": wait a minute and try again.
  • "You've been signed out": sign in again.
  • "Your payment was sent and is still being confirmed": don't pay again. Refresh the page in a minute.
  • New phone or computer: install Kaspire and restore your wallet with its recovery words. Your membership is still in it.
