Joining takes about a minute, once they have a wallet

This is what your members do. They never make an account or type a card number. Someone new to Kaspa first gets the free Kaspire wallet and buys some KAS. Our help page walks them through it.

  1. Get the Kaspire wallet

    It's free: an add-on for Chrome on a computer, or an app on an Android phone. There's no iPhone app yet, so iPhone owners join from a computer.

  2. Sign in with Kaspire

    Kaspire asks them to approve a short message, which proves the wallet is theirs. It costs nothing and moves no money.

  3. Pick a plan and approve

    Kaspire shows the exact amount before they say yes. The membership is in their wallet a few seconds later.

Questions

What is a membership on KasPass?

A small coin in the member's Kaspa wallet that records which plan they have and until when. Your site checks it to let them in. Because it's in their wallet, nobody can take it away, and there's no account to lose.

Do my members need to know about crypto?

They need the free Kaspire wallet and some KAS. The membership page walks them through it, and our help page explains each step in plain words. Buying KAS for the first time is the slowest part: an exchange can take a day or two to open an account.

Can members cancel?

Yes, any time. Nothing renews unless they've set KAS aside for it, and whatever isn't used goes back to their wallet. Ending a membership also gives back its 1 KAS deposit.

What happens if KAS goes up or down?

If you price in dollars or euros, members pay the KAS amount for that price on the day they pay. Your price stays steady; the KAS amount changes with the rate. If you raise a price, members who prepaid keep the old one for 30 days and are told.

How do I get my money, and turn it into dollars?

Press Withdraw in your dashboard and approve it in Kaspire: everything in your vault moves to your wallet. To turn it into dollars, send it to an exchange that trades KAS and sell it there. KAS goes up and down, so it can be worth more or less by the time you sell. Your dashboard has a payments file you can download for your records.

What do I have to trust KasPass with?

Never your money: payments go from the member's wallet to your vault, and only your wallet can take them out. You do rely on our server for three things: showing your page, working out today's KAS amount for a price in dollars (up to a limit fixed when the plan is made), and running auto-renewals and group access. The full list is in SECURITY.md.

What if KasPass shuts down?

Your vault and your members' memberships are on the Kaspa blockchain, not on our server, so they stay. The code is public, so you or anyone else can run the same site from it and withdraw as before. While no server is running, the membership page, sales at today's price, auto-renewals and the Telegram and Discord checks stop.

Which wallets work?

Kaspire, as an add-on for Chrome or the Android app. On an iPhone, members can sign in from a computer for now.

Who runs KasPass?

Is it ready, and is it safe?

KasPass is new. It's live on Kaspa's main network for Kaspa Vision, and new creators are set up with us, by hand. Your earnings and your members' memberships are on the Kaspa blockchain, not on our servers. The code is public on GitHub and has had AI security reviews, but not yet an outside audit.
