How it works

Joining takes three steps and about a minute.

1

Get the Kaspire wallet

A free browser extension and phone app for Kaspa. Already have it? Skip ahead.

2

Sign in with one tap

Kaspire signs a short message to prove the wallet is yours. It's free and sends nothing.

3

Choose and approve

Pick monthly, a commitment or lifetime, approve in Kaspire, and the pass is in your wallet.

For members

Pay your way, leave any time

  • Monthly, a commitment for a few months up front, or lifetime.
  • Auto-renew from a balance you top up. Whatever isn't used comes back if you cancel.
  • Pay from your phone with Kaspire mobile, or from any wallet by QR code.
  • NFT holders can get a lower price, checked at every renewal.
For creators

Your page, your look, paid straight to you

  • Price in dollars, euros or KAS. Members pay today's KAS amount.
  • Payments land in your own vault, which only your wallet can empty.
  • Levels, free trials, founding prices and sales, all from one dashboard.
  • Copy colors, fonts and logo from your website in one click.
  • Your own website or app can check who is a member.

Questions

What is a pass?

A small coin in your Kaspa wallet, locked by a covenant that records your membership: which plan, and until when. Sites check it to let you in.

What does it cost besides the price?

A refundable deposit held in the pass, and a tiny network fee for each payment. Kaspire shows every amount before you approve.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Turn off auto-renew to get your unused balance back, or close the pass to get its deposit back.

Which wallets work?

Kaspire, as a browser extension or on your phone. Many plans can also be paid from any wallet with a QR code.
