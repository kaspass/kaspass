Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026.

This page covers kaspass.app and the membership pages KasPass runs for creators. In short: no adverts, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and we keep as little as we can.

The contact form

Your name, how to reach you and your message are kept on our server so we can reply. Only we can read them. We delete them once your question is settled, and sooner if you ask. To ask, write to {contact}.

Visiting

No adverts, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The fonts come from this site, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else to show the page. Our server doesn't keep a record of your visits. To stop floods of requests, it holds your internet address in memory for a short while; it isn't written down and it's gone when the server restarts.

Your browser keeps a few settings for you, such as light or dark, which you can clear any time. A creator's private page link sets one cookie so its own links keep working.

Signing in with a wallet

Your wallet address is public on the Kaspa blockchain. When you sign in and hold, buy, claim or link a membership on a site, that site remembers your address so the creator's member list can show it. Signing in proves the wallet is yours; it never gives us your keys or lets us move your money.

Telegram and Discord

If you link one, we keep your account's id and name next to your wallet address, so the creator's group can let you in and remove you when your membership ends. You can unlink it any time.

Other services

To show names, check what a wallet holds and check today's prices, our server asks public Kaspa services (name, NFT, token and blockchain services) and price services. It sends them wallet addresses where it needs to, never your name or contact details.

The blockchain

Memberships and payments are recorded on the Kaspa blockchain. Anyone can see them there, and nobody, including us, can delete them.
