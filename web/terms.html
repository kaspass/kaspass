Terms

These terms apply to kaspass.app and to the membership pages KasPass runs for creators. By using them you agree to the points below.

New software

KasPass is new and has not had an outside security audit. It is provided as it is, without a promise that it is free of faults. The code is public on GitHub, and what you have to trust is listed in SECURITY.md.

Your money

KasPass never holds your money. Payments go from a member's wallet to the creator's vault on the Kaspa blockchain, and only the creator's wallet can take them out. A member's deposit and any KAS they set aside for renewals stay in their own membership.

Payments are final

Payments on the Kaspa blockchain can't be reversed or refunded by KasPass. Refunds are between the member and the creator. Members can stop renewals and take back unused KAS at any time.

The fee

KasPass keeps 2% of each payment, taken from the creator's share, and nothing on payments under 25 KAS. There is no setup fee and no monthly fee.

Creators

Creators are responsible for what they sell, for delivering it to their members, for their own taxes, and for following the law where they live.

Misuse

We may switch off a page that is used for fraud or anything illegal. The memberships and the creator's vault stay on the blockchain.
